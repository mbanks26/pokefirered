FireRed Rev 1 - Red, Leaf and Sabrina story test build

NEW STORY
Leaf automatically approaches Red and Sabrina at their first arrival from Route 1.
She knows both, shows exactly the Boulder and Cascade Badges, mentions Eevee,
and leaves without battling. Sabrina's follower resumes afterward. The scene
uses a persistent flag and does not need Oak's Parcel first.

Mother, Oak, Blue, Brock, Misty, Erika and Giovanni receive selective context.
Leaf has later conversations in Cerulean, Lavender and Saffron, including an
on-screen Water Stone/Vaporeon moment after Saffron is liberated.

Sabrina remains the official Saffron Gym Leader and battles Red herself.
Red appears alone against her. Original Marsh Badge/TM04 rewards remain;
Sabrina rejoins after victory. Loss, retry and save/load preserve her status.
Ordinary battles keep the polished combined artwork and original Red throw.

DEFERRED / MANUAL REVIEW
Pikachu and guaranteed shiny starters are not implemented in this story phase.
Original starter choices/shiny rules remain. Leaf's full battle team, friendly
challenge and League-era meetings remain future work. The original female
route stays available; expanded Leaf-family NPC scenes are limited to Red.
Manic EMU still needs art, pacing, ball alignment, full-inventory TM handling,
long-session and later-event checks. Existing follower surf/bike/cutscene hiding
and safe ledge regrouping remain. Safari/tutorial/link/multi/genuine doubles
are not covered by this regression.
